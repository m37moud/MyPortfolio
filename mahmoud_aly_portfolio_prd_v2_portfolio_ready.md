## 2. Product Summary

The portfolio website will present Mahmoud Aly as an experienced Android Developer with 5+ years of experience building scalable, user-friendly mobile applications with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, plus hands-on backend experience with Ktor and Spring Boot.

The site should help recruiters, hiring managers, and technical collaborators quickly understand:

- What Mahmoud builds
- The technologies he works with
- The scope of his professional experience
- The quality and range of his projects
- How to contact him or download his resume

The portfolio should feel credible, focused, and proof-driven rather than overly promotional.

---

## 3. Product Goals

### 3.1 Primary Goals

- Present Mahmoud clearly as a strong Android candidate for full-time roles and freelance work.
- Showcase real project scope across Android, cross-platform, and backend-integrated systems.
- Make technical strengths easy to scan for recruiters in under 60 seconds.
- Give engineering managers enough depth to assess architecture, stack, and delivery experience.
- Provide a polished central profile that supports LinkedIn, GitHub, and resume sharing.

### 3.2 Success Criteria

- A recruiter can identify Mahmoud's role, years of experience, and main stack within the first screen.
- A visitor can review work experience, project highlights, and skills within a few scrolls.
- Resume download and contact actions are always easy to find.
- Project content feels specific and believable, using concrete technologies and outcomes from the CV.

### 6.2 Tone of Content

The writing should be:

- Grounded in real experience
- Recruiter-friendly

## 7. Visual Design Direction

### 7.1 Design Intent

The website should feel like a premium engineering portfolio rather than a template. It should emphasize clarity, structure, and technical credibility.

### 7.2 Visual Style

- Dark-mode-first design is appropriate
- Midnight blue / deep navy palette is suitable
- Strong section hierarchy
- Clean card layouts
- Subtle motion and hover states
- Minimal decorative noise

### 7.3 Color Direction

Recommended palette:

- Background: `#02060E`
- Primary accent: `#0356C5`
- Primary text: `#FFFFFF`
- Secondary text: `rgba(255,255,255,0.72)`

### 7.4 Typography Direction

Recommended approach:

- Headings: a modern geometric or technical sans serif
- Body: a clean readable sans serif

Good pairings:

- Space Grotesk + Inter
- Sora + Inter
- Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter
